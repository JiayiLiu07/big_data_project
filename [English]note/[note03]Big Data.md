### 3. 4V Characteristics (Volume, Velocity, Variety, and Veracity)
1. `Volume`:

Definition: The scale of data is enormous, ranging from terabytes to petabytes or even exabytes.

Characteristics: The data volume is growing at an extremely rapid rate, making it difficult for traditional data processing tools and methods to cope.

2. `Velocity`:

Definition: The speed at which data is generated, transmitted, and processed is extremely high.

Characteristics: Data must be processed and analyzed in real time or near real time to meet business needs.

3. `Variety`:

Definition: Data types and sources are extremely diverse, including structured data (such as tables in databases), semi-structured data (such as log files and XML files), and unstructured data (such as text, images, and videos).

Characteristics: Multiple data types must be processed and analyzed to extract valuable information.

4. `Veracity`:

Definition: The quality and reliability of data.

Characteristics: Data accuracy and consistency are crucial, as poor-quality data can lead to erroneous analytical results and decisions.
